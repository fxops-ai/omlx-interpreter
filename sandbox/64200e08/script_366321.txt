
# 🍝 Spaghetti Bolognese for Two 🍝

This is a quick and comforting classic meal for two.

## ⏳ Prep Time: 5 minutes
## 👩‍🍳 Cook Time: 25 minutes
## Serves: 2

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

**For the Pasta:**
*   8 oz (about 2 servings) Spaghetti noodles
*   Salt, for boiling water

**For the Sauce:**
*   1 tbsp Olive Oil
*   1/2 medium Onion, finely chopped
*   100g Ground Beef (or Italian sausage), nicely browned and crumbled
*   1/2 cup crushed Tomato Sauce (or passata)
*   1/4 cup Milk or Beef Broth (optional, for richness)
*   1 tsp Dried Herbs (oregano, basil blend)
*   Salt and freshly ground Black Pepper, to taste
*   Grated Parmesan Cheese, for serving

---

## 👩‍🍳 Instructions

### 🍝 Step 1: Cook the Pasta
1.  Bring a large pot of salted water to a rolling boil.
2.  Add the spaghetti and cook according to package directions until *al dente* (firm to the bite).
3.  Drain the spaghetti thoroughly.

### 🍅 Step 2: Make the Sauce
1.  While the pasta cooks, heat the olive oil in a medium saucepan over medium heat.
2.  Add the chopped onion and sauté until translucent (about 3-4 minutes).
3.  Add the ground beef and cook, breaking it up with a spoon, until browned through (about 5-7 minutes). Drain any excess fat.
4.  Pour in the crushed tomato sauce, milk/broth (if using), dried herbs, salt, and pepper. Stir to combine.
5.  Bring to a gentle simmer, then reduce the heat to low. Let it simmer uncovered for about 10 minutes, allowing the flavors to meld.

### 🍽️ Step 3: Combine and Serve
1.  Drain the cooked spaghetti.
2.  Pour the sauce into the pot with the spaghetti and gently toss to coat everything evenly. If it seems too thick, add a splash of pasta water.
3.  Serve immediately in two bowls. Top generously with grated Parmesan cheese.

Enjoy your homemade spaghetti!
